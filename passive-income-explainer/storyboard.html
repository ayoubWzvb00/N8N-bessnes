How do rich people make money while they sleep?

Flat-Vector Science · storyboard · 8 beats · 28 s

Facts and numbers I assumed

  • 8-hour work day, paid once per hour (8 coins), to stand for "work for hours to earn money".
  • Rent: $1,500 a month from the property; dividends: $300 a quarter (paid in March and June). Six months of both adds up to $9,600 in the piggy bank.
  • Compounding example: $10,000 at 7% a year for 30 years. Reinvested, it grows to 10,000 × 1.07³⁰ = $76,123. If the earnings are spent instead, the invested balance stays at $10,000 (flat line). The 7% is an illustrative long-run figure, not a promise; the caption says so on screen.
  • The risk beat shows a widening shaded range of possible outcomes and a bumpy line after the asteroid hits, to say returns vary and can fall. No specific loss figure is claimed.
  • Dividends are paid quarterly by most US companies that pay them; rent is shown as monthly gross income (no costs or taxes).
