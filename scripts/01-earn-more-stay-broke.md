# Script 01 — The more you earn, the easier it becomes to stay broke

**الحالة:** بانتظار موافقة المستخدم
**الكلمات:** 215 (~75–78 ثانية بسرعة ~165–170 wpm)
**الزاوية:** عادات المال المعكوسة + علم نفس المال (Lifestyle inflation)

---

## السكربت النهائي (للتسجيل الصوتي)

> The more you earn, the easier it becomes to stay broke. Most people believe their money problems will disappear once they start making more. So they chase a raise, a better job, or a bigger income. But here's what most people don't understand. Your income was never what made you wealthy. What makes you wealthy is the gap between what you earn and what you spend. Imagine someone who goes from making $4,000 a month to $8,000. At first, it feels like they're finally getting ahead. But slowly, their life starts to upgrade. A nicer apartment. A newer car. More expensive habits that quietly become normal. Years later, they're earning twice as much, but still have nothing left at the end of the month. And the interesting part is that it rarely feels like a decision. Every upgrade seems small and reasonable on its own. But together, they turn every raise into a bigger lifestyle instead of more freedom. This is why so many people with high salaries still live paycheck to paycheck. Of course, there's nothing wrong with enjoying the money you make. The problem starts when your expenses grow every time your income does. Because becoming wealthy isn't about how much money comes in. It's about how much of it stays with you.

## لماذا سينجح (مقارنة بالمرجع الذهبي)
- **هوك المفارقة** بنفس قالب الفيديو 1: *The more you X, the easier Y* — يتناقض مع أكبر افتراض عند الناس: "دخل أكبر = مشاكل أقل".
- **مرآة شخصية قوية للجمهور الأمريكي**: "paycheck to paycheck" عبارة يعرفها كل أمريكي.
- **قانون واحد قابل للاقتباس**: *wealth = the gap between what you earn and what you spend*.
- **مفارقة ثانية في المنتصف**: "it rarely feels like a decision" — التضخم لا يحدث بقرار بل بتراكم قرارات صغيرة.
- **قائمة ثلاثية مرتين**: (a raise, a better job, a bigger income) و (apartment, car, habits).
- **حلقة مغلقة**: الهوك "stay broke" ← النهاية "stays with you" (نفس الفعل *stay* معكوس المعنى) + تنتهي بـ **you**.
