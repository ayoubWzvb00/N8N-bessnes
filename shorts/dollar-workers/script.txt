Every dollar you spend is gone. Every dollar you keep goes to work.
Most people think money is simple: you earn it, you spend it. But wealthy people see it differently. To them, every dollar is a worker.
Imagine two people with the same salary. The first spends everything that comes in. Every month starts from zero. The second keeps a small part of every paycheck and puts it to work. Those dollars bring back more dollars. And those new dollars go to work too.
They earn exactly the same. But only one of them is building a team.
When you spend a dollar, it works once, for someone else. When you keep it and put it to work, it can work for you for years. Even while you sleep.
That's why wealthy people don't ask, "Can I afford this?" They ask, "What could this dollar become?"
Of course, your first workers will be small. For a while, it will feel like nothing is happening. But every team starts with one worker. And it grows one dollar at a time.
Because getting rich isn't about working for money forever. It's about building a team of money that works for you.
